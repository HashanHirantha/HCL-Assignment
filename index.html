Frequently Asked Questions (Troubleshooting)

How do I enroll in elective modules for this semester?
Navigate to the Course Catalog tab, locate your degree program and academic year, click the elective module, and enter the self-enrollment key provided during the dean's orientation session.
My assignment submission failed or timed out. What should I do?
Make sure your file is below the 25 MB limit and formatted as a `.zip` or `.pdf`. If an error persists, take a timestamped screenshot and email it directly to your module coordinator before the 11:59 PM deadline.
How do I reset my institutional Google/SJP student password?
Visit the USJ central identity portal at cit.sjp.ac.lk/pwreset using your student registration number and alternate mobile verification code.
